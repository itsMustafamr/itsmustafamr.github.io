## Recent & Ongoing Research
- **Project 1 – Tabular Foundation Models**  
  Summary: Exploring TabPFN, TabICL, TabDPT, and related in-context learning approaches for robust tabular understanding.  
  Collaborators: Dr. Aditya, Dr. Adarsh  
  Status: Ongoing

- **Project 2 – CRAFT: Text-to-Parametric CAD**  
  Summary: A corrective and robust multi-agent framework that turns natural language into editable, parametric CAD. It combines semantic understanding, parametric planning, compilation, and rendering with self-correction and component-level verification, using multi-view visual feedback to detect and repair geometric errors.  
  Collaborators: Dr. Aditya, Dr. Adarsh, Dr. Chinmay  
  Status: **Accepted at ICLAD 2026**  
  Project page: [idealab-isu.github.io/CRAFT](https://idealab-isu.github.io/CRAFT/) · Code: [idealab-isu/CRAFT](https://github.com/idealab-isu/CRAFT)  
  Poster: Presented at [NVIDIA GTC 2026](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-qs7N_WnKxkOgNpsu7IW01a5wmsZcqZM/view?usp=sharing), San Jose, CA (March 2026)  
  Paper: *CRAFT: Corrective and Robust Multi-Agent Framework for Text-to-Parametric CAD*, ICLAD 2026 (accepted May 2026)

- **Project 3 – VISTA-CAD**  
  Summary: A follow-up to CRAFT on iteratively refining parametric CAD programs using visual feedback.  
  Collaborators: Dr. Aditya, Dr. Adarsh, Dr. Chinmay  
  Status: Ongoing — manuscript in preparation
